How Your Garden Grows

1. Finish the Mini Game and earn Growth Points.

2. Click an empty plot and buy a seed.

3. Click the watering can to activate it.

4. Click a plant to water it. Click the watering can again to stop.

Every watering changes the plant artwork and moves it to the next stage. Larger plants need more stages.
